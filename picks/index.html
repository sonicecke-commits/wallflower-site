Questions

Before you hang it.

Can I hang a bike with hydraulic disc brakes?

Shimano's manual says its disc brakes aren't designed to work with the bike upside down or on its side, and asks you to squeeze each lever a few times before you ride. SRAM, quoted by Steadyrack, says vertical storage "should not create any harm to the brakes".

If a lever feels soft after storage, have a bike shop check it. To keep the bike level, use a floor stand or a sideways wall mount.

Sources: Shimano user's manual · Steadyrack, quoting SRAM

I ride an e-bike. What should I check?

E-bikes are heavy. Check the mount's weight limit against your bike's weight before you buy.

How we pick

How a mount gets listed.

Picks checked November 12, 2026. Prices change, so check them on Amazon.

  1. Fit first

    We sort by the space and the bike, using each maker's own description of what it holds.

  2. Complaints read

    We read owner complaints and product recall lists for safety problems, and leave out anything that looks risky.

  3. Ratings bar

    4.5 stars or more from at least 150 ratings when we check. Two living-room mounts have fewer ratings, and their cards say so.

  4. Empty tiers

    If nothing passes in a tier, we leave it empty.

Find the stud first.

Any wall mount is only as strong as what it's screwed into. A cheap stud finder, two lag screws into wood, and your landlord's OK if you rent.
