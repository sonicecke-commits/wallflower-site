Bikes look good on walls.

Cafés and bike shops figured this out years ago. A bike on the wall reads as something you chose, not something in the way.

Wallflower brings that to a one-bedroom flat: one mount, one bike, and the floor back.

How much will it cost?

We haven't set a price. The waitlist hears first.

Why not e-bikes?

E-bikes often weigh 20-30 kg. We won't rate the first kit for that until it's tested.

I can't drill. Anything for me?

We're looking at a lean-on stand that needs no holes. Pick "I rent" in the checker and tell us in the sign-up form, so we know how many people want it.

Why are the drawings drawings?

Wallflower is run with AI help. The drawings and videos are AI-made illustrations, and the photos on this page are stock photos of real homes and shops, not our product. Real photos of the kit come when there's a real kit.

Privacy

Effective date: October 30, 2026. This covers the waitlist only. Nothing is on sale yet, and this will be replaced with a fuller policy before anything is.

Wallflower is a one-person business in Boulder, Colorado. This explains what I collect when you join the waitlist, why, and who else sees it.

What I collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your answers to the optional questions, including your fit-checker answers if you used it.
  • Which link brought you here (for example, from Instagram or TikTok), so I can see which posts work.
  • When you signed up and confirmed, and whether you open or click my emails (my email tool records this).
  • If you email me: your address and what you write.

Why

To email you when the float kit is ready to buy, to learn which posts and options interest people (I look at totals, not at you personally), and to answer you if you write. I won't send other emails unless you choose to get more.

Who else sees it

I don't sell your personal information and I don't share it for advertising. These services handle it for me:

  • MailerLite runs the sign-up form, stores the list and sends the email. Its servers may be in the European Union.
  • Google (Gmail) holds my business email.
  • Anthropic: I use an AI assistant (Claude) to help run the business. It may read sign-up numbers and messages you send me.
  • GitHub hosts this site and Google Fonts serves its fonts. They receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

The site has no analytics or advertising trackers of its own. MailerLite's form script may set cookies so the form works.

How long I keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it. After you unsubscribe I keep only a note of your address so I don't email it again.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe with the link in any email.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion by emailing wallflower.bikes@gmail.com. I aim to reply within 10 business days.
  • Don't sell my information: I don't sell it, but you can still send that request (for example, under Nevada law) to the same address.
  • Do Not Track: this site doesn't track you across other sites, so a Do Not Track signal changes nothing here.

Children

The waitlist isn't meant for children under 13. If a child has signed up, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this changes, the effective date above changes too. If a change affects how your information is used, I'll say so by email before it applies.
